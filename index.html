About the School
No Franchise. Ever.
Richard Dale

Passion-Driven.
Industry-Led.

Richard Dale Theatre School is a professional independent stage school — not a franchise — specialising in triple threat training across acting, singing, and dancing.

With over 120 students drawn from schools across the South West, we maintain intentionally small class sizes to ensure every student receives the focused, professional training they deserve. No babysitting. No tea breaks. Just serious craft.

Each year we bring West End performers directly into the studio — giving students firsthand access to working industry professionals from productions including Mamma Mia, Newsies, and more.

Patrons of the School
Anthony Drewe & Judi Spiers
The Training

Three Disciplines.
One Performer.

🎭

Acting

From scene study to audition technique, students develop the emotional truth and technical skill to inhabit any role convincingly on stage or screen.

🎤

Singing

Vocal development rooted in musical theatre repertoire — belt, mix, and classical styles explored with care for the student's individual voice.

💃

Dancing

Technique across contemporary, jazz, and musical theatre styles, building the physical command and performance quality demanded by top drama schools.
